About KurdNxt

Who we are.

KurdNxt is a community for the holistic development of the next generation — supporting young people in education, personal growth, career and social engagement while connecting them with people and possibilities.

Our story

One place for what comes next.

KurdNxt grew from a simple question: with so many organisations, initiatives, educational offers and talented people already out there, why is it still so difficult for a young person to know where to go?

We want to build a central place where a student can ask for school support, a family can find orientation, a university student can find a study group or internship, and a young professional can meet people with similar ambitions.

KurdNxt grew from the ideas of young university students and a shared belief that, as a community, we can share far more knowledge, opportunities and experiences with one another. The goal is to build a place that grows with its community and is shaped by the people who are part of it — For our youth. By our youth.

We do not see a “problematic generation”.
We see enormous potential that can be connected and supported much better.

Our mission

To make knowledge, orientation, networks and opportunities more accessible; connect people who can learn from and support one another; and help young people turn ambition into concrete next steps.

Our vision

We want to see realised dreams — and new, bigger dreams growing from them. A generation represented wherever it wants to be: in education, trades, science, medicine, engineering, entrepreneurship, culture, technology, public service and beyond.

Success at KurdNxt should be passed on. Students become mentors. People who once needed guidance return with experience. Doors that opened for one person stay open for the next.

Our values

What we build by.

Ambition

Big goals deserve to be taken seriously — no matter where someone starts.

Opportunity

Talent should not lose out simply because someone did not know a path existed.

Community

Share knowledge, celebrate success and leave doors open for the next person.

Growth

No one is finished. It is never too late to learn, change direction or begin again.

Roots

Move forward with confidence in where we come from and openness to the world.

Give Back

Take support when you need it. Give it when you can.

Independent. Open. Together.

KurdNxt is party-politically independent and not affiliated with any political party or movement. It is religiously neutral and respects people of different faiths as well as people without religion. Our Kurdish roots are central to our story; our support is not restricted by ethnicity.

We start in Germany. We build with a wider future in mind.
