Why KurdNxt

Potential is everywhere.
Access is not.

Talented young Kurds live across different countries, cities and communities. Too often, they lack the networks, information and spaces that make it easier to move forward.

KurdNxt exists to make those connections visible — between students, mentors, professionals, opportunities and each other.

What we want to build

Four pillars. One community.

01

Education

Study support, workshops, academic guidance and spaces where young people can learn from one another.

02

Mentoring

Connecting students and young professionals with people who can share experience, guidance and perspective.

03

Community

Building stronger links across cities, countries and generations — without losing identity along the way.

04

Opportunity

Making scholarships, internships, events, volunteering and career pathways easier to discover and access.

Our vision
“A generation that does not only ask where it belongs — but knows what it can build.”

KurdNxt aims to become a platform where Kurdish youth across Europe and Kurdistan can exchange knowledge, create projects, open doors for each other and grow together.

Starting small. Thinking long-term.
