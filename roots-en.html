What it means.

Roots makes Kurdish language, history, culture, literature and connection to Kurdistan accessible to a younger generation — while collaborating with organisations already doing valuable work.

The vision

Summer schools, language programmes, international youth projects and structured exchange and volunteer programmes between Europe and Kurdistan.
